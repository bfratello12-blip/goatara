How it works

From hello to your first payment in ~4 weeks

A clear, simple path with no surprises. Here's exactly what happens from the moment we meet to the day your first payout lands.

Day1

Get to know each other

We hop on a call to understand your products, your goals, and where you're selling today — if you're selling anywhere yet. No obligation, just a conversation to see if we're a fit.

Week1

Marketing plan

We build a tailored marketing plan: how your store will be positioned, which products to lead with, and the advertising strategy we'll run to drive sales.

Week2

Website build & ad setup

We build your Shopify store — or take over the one you already have — and set up the ad accounts. We fund the advertising and the tools; you just cover your own Shopify subscription.

Week3

Begin running

Your store goes live and the ads start running. Orders come in, you ship the products, and we manage and optimise everything behind the scenes.

Week4

First payment

You receive your first monthly payout. Your first month is free — we take no revenue share — so 100% of what the store generated goes to you. From there it's the standard 60/40 split, month after month.

The ongoing rhythm

A simple monthly split, paid to you

Once you're running, the routine is easy: we run and fund the marketing, you ship the orders, and every month the revenue is split.

  • Your first month is free. We take 0% of month one, so you keep all of it.
  • You receive 60% of revenue, distributed monthly.
  • We keep 40% to cover our work, the ad spend, and the tools we run.
  • No hidden costs. If the store doesn't earn, you don't owe us anything.
