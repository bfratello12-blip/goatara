The honest version

Why would we pay for all of this?

It's a fair question. Here's exactly how we make money — there's nothing hidden underneath it.

1

We take the upfront risk

The build, the Shopify fees, the ad accounts, and the ad spend all come out of our pocket. You're not paying us to try.

2

We take 40% of what it earns

That 40% is our entire payment. It has to cover our team, the software, and every ad dollar we spent getting the sale.

3

No sales, no payday

If the store doesn't sell, we've spent our money for nothing and you owe us nothing. That's why we're picky about who we work with — and why we work hard once we say yes.
